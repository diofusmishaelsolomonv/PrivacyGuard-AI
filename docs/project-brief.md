# PrivacyGuard AI — Project Brief

## Problem

Sensitive information is frequently displayed on laptops, but conventional privacy controls do not necessarily understand who is viewing the screen or how sensitive the current context is.

## Solution

PrivacyGuard AI proposes a context-aware privacy layer that combines camera-based identity context with screen-content sensitivity and applies a graded privacy response.

## Core Pipeline

**Who → What → Context → Risk → Action**

## Current Prototype Scope

- Authorized-user verification concept
- Sensitive-content detection
- Adaptive privacy engine
- OTP privacy protection in a controlled demo application
- Live privacy shield
- Shoulder-surfing protection
- Privacy event log

## Target Platform

Snapdragon-powered PCs with on-device AI.

## AI Strategy

The architecture is designed around local AI inference for privacy-sensitive signals. Qualcomm AI Hub-compatible model concepts are considered for the vision pipeline.

## Innovation

The key idea is not simply detecting a face or detecting sensitive text. The system combines **identity context and content sensitivity** to choose an appropriate privacy action.

## Future Vision

The longer-term system can become an autonomous privacy agent capable of predicting privacy risk, understanding application/context signals and adapting protection policies across devices and organizations.

## Status

Prototype under development. Proposed architecture and target Snapdragon deployment are documented separately from future capabilities.
